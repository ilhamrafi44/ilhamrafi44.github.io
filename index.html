01

ISP Subscriber Platform

PT Surya Bestari Lestari · SBLNET

An entire ISP's subscriber lifecycle in one platform: onboarding, automated network provisioning, recurring billing, double-entry accounting, and payment collection across multiple gateways plus a direct host-to-host bank integration.

  • 27-module modular monolith on Spring Modulith — hexagonal / DDD, event-driven boundaries
  • 8 PostgreSQL databases, per-context ACID via multi-datasource transaction management
  • Double-entry General Ledger; money as integer cents, because floats and money are a horror film
  • Pluggable gateway port: Duitku (VA / QRIS / e-wallet) plus host-to-host BRI Fixed VA
  • FreeRADIUS + MikroTik RouterOS — PPPoE, live sessions, CoA disconnect, dunning auto-isolation
